Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Overview

OpenClaw Personal Assistant is a private, self-hosted application used by authorized individuals to manage personal information and digital services.

2. Information Accessed

With user authorization, the application may access Gmail information, including email messages, message metadata, labels, and attachments, depending on the permissions granted.

3. How Information Is Used

Gmail information is used to provide requested functionality, such as searching emails, summarizing messages, organizing correspondence, and assisting with email management.

4. Data Storage

The application operates on a privately managed server. Authentication credentials and configuration data are stored locally with restricted access.

Email content may be processed by configured AI service providers when necessary to fulfill user requests. Such processing is subject to the applicable provider terms and privacy practices.

5. Data Sharing

Gmail information is not sold or used for advertising. Information is shared with service providers only as necessary to provide the application's requested functionality.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information is retained as necessary for the application's operation. Authorized users may request deletion of locally stored information and revoke Google Account access at any time.

7. Google API Services

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

8. Security

The application uses OAuth authentication and restricted local access to protect credentials and user information.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application administrator through the contact information provided in the OAuth consent screen.
