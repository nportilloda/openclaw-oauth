OpenClaw Personal Assistant

OpenClaw Personal Assistant is a private, self-hosted application designed to help its authorized users manage personal information and everyday tasks.

Gmail Integration

The application connects to Gmail through Google OAuth 2.0 with the user's authorization.

Depending on the permissions granted, the assistant may access email messages and metadata to help users search, organize, and manage their email.

Access is restricted to authorized users. The application is not offered as a public service.

Data Privacy

Gmail access is controlled through Google OAuth. Users can revoke access through their Google Account settings.

For more information, please read our Privacy Policy.
